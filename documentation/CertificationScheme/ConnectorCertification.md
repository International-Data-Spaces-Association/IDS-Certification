# Part 2 - Connector certification
To secure the intended data sharing, Connectors must provide technical interoperability and trust in identity and identity management, complying with current standards. As such, Connector certification is interoperability- and trust-focused, while aiming to strengthen the development and maintenance process of these components.

## Modules
The IDS certification follows a modular approach. Participants can choose which module they want to get certified in. 

### Module 1: Technical Interoperability

Technical interoperability in data spaces primarily encompasses the Dataspace Protocol (DSP) and semantic models. The DSP forms the foundation for interoperability among data space participants.

Connector testing and certification cover four key components of the DSP:

- Common Requirements
- Data Catalog Protocol
- Contract Negotiation Protocol
- Transfer Process Protocol

In addition, Module 1 evaluates two further dimensions of technical interoperability:

- Communication Integrity
- Data Usage Control

Module 1 is based on the corresponding Technology Compatibility Kit (DSP-TCK) and is the top-priority module for Connector certification.

### Module 2: Trust

Identity and Access Management is a critical component of Connector trust, encompassing identity definition, issuance, authentication and authorization management. The Decentralized Claims Protocol (DCP) provides a standardized mechanism for issuing and verifying decentralized identity claims. The use of DCP is mandatory to ensure consistent issuance and verification of identity attributes across Connectors.

Module 2 includes:

- Identity and Access Management
- Identification and Authentication Control

Module 2 is based on the corresponding Technology Compatibility Kit (DCP-TCK). The test specifications for Module 2 are under development.

## Connector Certification Criteria Catalog

The Connector Certification Criteria Catalog consolidates all criteria applied during the evaluation of a Connector under IDS Certification. It contains the individual requirements for both Module 1 (Technical Interoperability) and Module 2 (Trust), providing a single, consistent reference for applicants, Evaluation Facilities and the Certification Body throughout the certification process.

For Module 1, the catalog specifies the test cases derived from the DSP-TCK, covering common requirements, the Data Catalog Protocol, the Contract Negotiation Protocol, the Transfer Process Protocol, communication integrity and data usage control. For Module 2, it specifies the requirements derived from the DCP-TCK, covering identity and access management and identification and authentication control.

Because certification can be pursued for Module 1 only, Module 2 only or a combined certification covering both modules, the catalog is structured so that the applicant and the Evaluation Facility can select and apply only the criteria relevant to the chosen certification configuration.

Maintenance and versioning of the catalog is the responsibility of the Certification Body, in cooperation with IDSA and the Evaluation Facilities, so that it stays aligned with the evolving Dataspace Protocol (DSP) and Decentralized Claims Protocol (DCP) specifications and their respective Technology Compatibility Kits.
